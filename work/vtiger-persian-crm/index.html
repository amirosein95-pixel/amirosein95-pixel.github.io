Vtiger CRM, localized for a Persian business

A CRM that works the way a Persian-speaking team works: Jalali dates, right-to-left screens, SMS and phone calls in one place.

Type
Software
Year
2026
Result
The company CRM, used by 80+ people.

The situation

The optical and contact-lens division of Noor Eye Hospital needed one CRM for its customer relationships. The staff work in Persian and plan by the Jalali calendar, so the system had to do the same — throughout, not as an add-on.

The decision: adapt, don’t build

I compared building a CRM from scratch with adapting an open-source one. Vtiger already covered the core of a CRM, so the effort could go into what was missing for Persian-speaking users. I chose to adapt it.

What I changed

  • The Jalali calendar throughout the system.
  • A full Persian translation and a right-to-left layout.
  • SMS integration.
  • PBX Manager, to log and track customer calls.
  • Background workers that keep customer statuses up to date automatically.

Result

It is now the company’s main CRM, used by 80+ people.

For anyone planning something similar

Localization is more than translation. Dates, text direction, messaging and telephony all have to work together, so plan for all of them from the start.

The same approach applies to any business that needs open-source software to work in a right-to-left language or a non-Gregorian calendar.
